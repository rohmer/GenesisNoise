 The line segment making the Voronoi cells, i.e. the points equidistant to the two nearest Voronoi sites. These are the lines in the VoronoiSite.Cell. This has VoronoiPoint end points, i.e. Start and End. This has the two VoronoiSites they separate, i.e. Right and Left.
